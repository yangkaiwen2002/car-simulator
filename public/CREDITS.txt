ADAPTATIONS IN OPENROAD
Original JOE v3 browser loader; winding converted to counterclockwise;
texture upload orientation adjusted; paint alpha interpreted as paint mask;
rim dimensions adapted to the .car tire specification; tire geometry generated;
transparent glass material and browser camera positions adapted.
Engine sample playback rate varies with simulated RPM. Samples are community
assets, not verified recordings of each factory vehicle.
The shared source steering-wheel model is not a separate factory wheel per car.
Original urban environment and website interface.
Functional gauge faces added to TC6 and MI blank dashboard areas, including
speed, RPM, gear and on MI session time / throttle / brake. This is a simulation
adaptation, not a factory instrument replica. MI steering position adjusted
to avoid intersection with its dashboard.

PHYSICS AND REFERENCE PROJECTS
VDrift: https://github.com/VDrift/vdrift
JOE format and .car parameter semantics were studied to load the original assets.
Sketchbook: https://github.com/swift502/Sketchbook
Car.ts was studied for browser control patterns, steering smoothing, gear
transitions and first-person interaction. Its implementation is not bundled.
The OpenRoad physics solver is original, not the full solver from either project.
It uses a flat-road bicycle model, torque interpolation, automatic gears,
aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance and simplified traction/braking limits.
No suspension dynamics, wheel slip model, drivetrain elasticity, damage,
manufacturer validation, traffic AI or collision rigid-body solver is included.
All displayed performance figures are community configuration values.
