Original asset files preserved. Runtime material, scale, wheel, camera and damage adaptations by OpenRoad.
The .car file is an original approximate gameplay calibration, not factory measurements.
Torque curves, gear ratios, mass, brake settings, tire grip and aero are calibrated estimates.
Hybrid propulsion uses a single combined torque curve; no battery or energy-recovery simulation.
Engine audio is a placeholder from EF/CO, not a recording of this car.
