OpenRoad adaptations: baked scene transforms; 4.794 m length; vertex welding
at 0.1 mm tolerance, with all 1,492,320 source triangles retained; original
steering assembly separated by connected components; wheel and caliper motion;
paint, cabin and glass materials; camera position and body damage integration.
Geometry buffer reduced from 120,695,328 to 54,525,868 bytes. Original textures
are preserved. Not a scan or a factory-certified interior. Dashboard and center
screen artwork are static; live speed/RPM use the HUD. Mirrors are not live.

Simulation represents the 2021 M4 Competition RWD (not the newer facelift).
375 kW, 650 Nm, 2,993 cc, 8 gears, factory ratios/final drive, wheelbase and
base tire sizes checked against BMW March 2021 technical specifications:
https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/attachment/T0326324EN/472231
Mass 1,800 kg approximates the EU specification including driver.
Torque interpolation, inertia, brakes, tire grip and aero are game tuning.
The sound is a CS community placeholder, not a factory S58 recording.
